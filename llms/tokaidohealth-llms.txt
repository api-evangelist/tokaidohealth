# Tokaido Health

> Tokaido Health is an AI-native medication steerage platform anchored in behavioral economics. It helps employers and health plans cut pharmacy costs by identifying same-or-better medications that cost less — site-of-care steerage, biosimilar substitution, formulary optimization, and polypharmacy reconciliation — and pairing those recommendations with incentives that get members to switch. The platform layers on top of the existing PBM and benefits stack with no PBM swap or plan design overhaul. Founded in 2025, headquartered in San Mateo, California; came out of stealth in May 2026 with $25M in funding led by Norwest Venture Partners, with Primary Venture Partners, Next Ventures, Constellation, and Scrub Capital.
